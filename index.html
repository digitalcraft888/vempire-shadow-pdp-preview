SHADOW moves through darkness with sharp contrast. Featuring a black sunburst dial with red accents, a 316L stainless steel case and bracelet, and a scratch-resistant sapphire crystal — an expression of quiet authority and everyday precision.

Case

The 42.5 mm case in 316L stainless steel features a scratch-resistant sapphire crystal and a brushed-and-polished finish. The timepiece is water-resistant to 5 ATM.

  • Water Resistance 5 ATM

Dial

The black sunburst dial is complemented by red-accented hands and Roman numeral hour-markers filled with luminescent material, framed by a 60-second subdial at 6 o'clock.

  • Dial Opening 30.5 mm
  • Hands Luminous

Bracelet

The 316L stainless steel bracelet is fitted with a quick-release interchangeable system, secured by a butterfly clasp for a comfortable, balanced fit.

  • Clasp Butterfly, Quick-Release
